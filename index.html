About Me

Profile

I am an IT student who enjoys turning manual, paper-based processes into working software. My school projects range from a resort booking platform to a collection management system for ICSA, and outside class I build small, focused services — authentication, rate limiting, pagination, and media uploads — to understand how production backends are put together.
